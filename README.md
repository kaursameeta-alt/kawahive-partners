# KawaHive & Partners

KawaHive & Partners provides go-to-market (GTM) strategy for founders with registered MedTech and medical devices in APAC, focusing on hospital demand, distributor sell-through and customer orders.

KawaHive helps medical device manufacturers understand hospital and customer demand across APAC, prioritise target accounts, understand hospital buying behaviour, strengthen distributor performance and convert market interest into customer orders.
