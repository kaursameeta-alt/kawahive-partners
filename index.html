10-Week Go-to-Market Sprint

US$3,500

For founders with a registered MedTech or medical device product or service who need dedicated support to work through one primary go-to-market challenge, focus on the right actions and make decisions based on customer and market evidence.

What we do together

  1. Diagnose: Identify the root cause of the GTM challenge and what is preventing progress.
  2. Prioritise: Decide what needs attention first and where to focus limited time and resources.
  3. Plan: Build a practical action plan with clear priorities, actions and evidence to gather.
  4. Execute & monitor: Work through agreed actions, review progress each week and assess customer and market feedback.
  5. Pivot: Make timely, evidence-informed adjustments when the results show that the approach needs to change.

What's included

  • 10 weeks of structured, dedicated 1:1 online support.
  • One 60-minute online session each week.
  • Focus on one primary GTM challenge throughout the sprint.
  • Weekly action reviews, progress monitoring and strategic adjustments.

By week 10: practical outputs
Depending on the agreed GTM challenge, you will have worked towards:

  • A documented diagnosis of the primary GTM challenge and the evidence behind it.
  • A prioritised view of the markets, customer accounts, buying stakeholders or distributor issues that need attention first.
  • A focused action plan with clear actions, owners, timelines and progress measures.
  • A record of actions taken, customer or distributor feedback, and what the evidence indicates.
  • An end-of-sprint review setting out what is working, what remains unresolved and whether to continue, change approach or stop investing in the current path.
  • Recommended next priorities for the following 90 days.

The outputs are tailored to one primary challenge. The sprint supports better decisions and disciplined execution; it does not guarantee sales, purchase orders or market-entry results.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are you a regulatory consultancy?

No. We do not prepare regulatory submissions or provide regulatory consulting or compliance services. We focus on go-to-market decisions: customer prioritisation, market choices, distributor performance, positioning and the steps that can turn regulatory progress into customer traction.

Are you a marketing agency?

No. We do not sell marketing campaigns or act as an outsourced marketing team. We help you understand the go-to-market problem first — for example, why hospital interest is not converting into purchase orders, or why a distributor is not generating sustained demand — and clarify which decisions to address next.

Do I need to have chosen my country and distributor?

No. We can help you assess your options. The free prospecting toolkit will be most useful once you have a target market or customer segment in mind.

What are your payment and cancellation terms?

Payment for the 10-week GTM Sprint is due in full upfront and is non-refundable. If you need to cancel a scheduled session, please give at least 24 hours’ notice. A cancellation made at least 24 hours before the session will not incur a no-show fee. Cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice or missed sessions may incur a no-show fee.
