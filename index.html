Commercial decision
Shift focus, support tender specifications and pursue the stronger first breakthrough opportunity.

    FAQ

    Direct answers.

    Are you a regulatory consultancy?

    No. We do not handle submissions or compliance. Our work begins on the commercial side: customers, positioning, channels, markets and enablement.

    How are you different from a marketing agency?

    The starting point is the commercial problem, not the campaign. We look at the product, customer, channel and market together before deciding what communication or action is actually needed.

    Do you replace an internal Clinical Applications team?

    No. Clinical Applications teams are critical for product and clinical support. KawaHive looks more broadly at the commercial question: what is stopping adoption, customer traction or market growth?

    Do you work with commercial partners?

    Yes. Partner capability, market fit, commercial confidence and customer-facing execution can be important parts of a MedTech market-entry or growth situation.

    When should we engage?

    When the product is ready — or already in market — but there is uncertainty around customer demand, positioning, market choice, competitive pressure, partner strategy or the next commercial move.
