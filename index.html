A straightforward process

How we work

We begin with your issue, agree how to handle confidential information, and only then decide whether focused support makes sense.

01

Free 30-minute discovery session

You tell us what is happening. We listen, ask clarifying questions and map the commercial issue. We will tell you honestly whether we can help.

02

Agree confidentiality if needed

If sensitive information needs to be shared, we agree an NDA before confidential information is shared.

03

Agree on paid advisory work

If there is a clear fit, we propose focused, paid one-to-one support for the specific issue. Before any work begins, we agree the scope, approach and fee with you. There is no obligation to proceed.

04

Choose whether to continue

Once the agreed paid work is complete, we review what is needed next. If further paid sessions or ongoing retainer support would help, we discuss the options and fees upfront. You decide whether to continue.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are you a regulatory consultancy?

No. We do not handle regulatory submissions or compliance. We focus on the market side: customers, channels and positioning for MedTech and medical device businesses.

How are you different from a marketing agency?

We start with the market problem, not the campaign. First we listen and work to understand what is holding back commercial progress; then we consider the next step.

Do I need to have chosen my country and distributor?

No. We can help you assess your options. The free prospecting toolkit will be most useful once you have a target market or customer segment in mind.
