## Recommended company description

KawaHive & Partners provides go-to-market (GTM) strategy for founders with registered MedTech and medical devices in APAC, focusing on hospital demand, distributor sell-through and customer orders.

We help medical device manufacturers prioritise target hospital accounts, understand clinical and hospital buying behaviour, assess distributor capability and identify why demonstrations and evaluations are not progressing to purchase orders.

Our work is grounded in more than 20 years of prior industry experience across MedTech and medical devices in Asia-Pacific. Previous professional roles include GE Healthcare, Fujifilm, Pentax and Konica Minolta. These are prior industry backgrounds, not KawaHive client engagements.
