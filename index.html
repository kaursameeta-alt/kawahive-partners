How we assess

Look beyond the symptom.

Most commercial conversations stay at the surface. We dig into the real blockage — customer fit, market choice, buying behaviour or distributor capability — and identify the evidence that would change the next decision.

How we dig into the problem

We focus on go-to-market decisions between regulatory clearance and sustained purchase orders — not marketing campaigns or regulatory filings.

Once we know where progress is stuck, we examine the underlying commercial factors:

  • Who has the clearest need and a reason to buy now?
  • Which market, segment or account deserves priority first?
  • What actually drives the buying decision beyond clinical interest?
  • Can the distributor reach, support and develop the right customers — or only take opening orders?

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are you a regulatory consultancy?

No. We do not prepare regulatory submissions or provide regulatory consulting or compliance services. We focus on the commercial side: customer prioritisation, market choices, channel performance, positioning and the decisions that can help turn regulatory progress into market traction.

Are you a marketing agency?

No. We do not sell marketing campaigns or act as an outsourced marketing team. We help you understand the commercial problem first — for example, why hospital interest is not converting into purchase orders, or why a distributor is not generating sustained demand — and clarify which decisions to address next.

Do I need to have chosen my country and distributor?

No. We can help you assess your options. The free prospecting toolkit will be most useful once you have a target market or customer segment in mind.

What are your payment and cancellation terms?

Payment for advisory sessions and packages is due in full upfront and is non-refundable. If you need to cancel a scheduled session, please give at least 24 hours’ notice. A cancellation made at least 24 hours before the session will not incur a no-show fee. Cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice or missed sessions may incur a no-show fee.
