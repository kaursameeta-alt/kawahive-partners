10-Week Go-to-Market Sprint

US$3,500 · Fixed fee

For founders with a registered product or service whose go-to-market problem is costing orders. We diagnose what is holding orders back, decide what to fix first and test the fix against real customer or distributor evidence.

If the evidence says a market, account or distributor relationship is not worth pursuing, we will tell you. We will not recommend continuing just to keep the activity going.

Who it is for

Founders with a product or service authorised in their target market who need a clearer route to customer orders. This includes teams with hospital or distributor activity but few orders, and teams approved but without market contact.

Not suited to: Companies without a product or service yet, companies awaiting approval, or those seeking regulatory, clinical-evidence or marketing services. If approval is pending, start with the free Lab tools and Insights.

To explore the sprint, start with the free 30-minute GTM Clarity Call. We confirm suitability, the primary challenge, start date and terms in writing before paid work begins.

How the ten weeks run

  • Weeks 1–2 · Diagnose: Review information and agree one primary challenge.
  • Weeks 3–4 · Prioritise: Rank accounts, markets or distributor issues using evidence, not opinion. If there is no market contact, define a target list and agree progress signals.
  • Weeks 5–8 · Test: You carry out agreed actions. We review actual customer or distributor behaviour and feedback, then adjust priorities.
  • Weeks 9–10 · Decide: A written review of what worked, what did not and whether evidence supports continuing, changing or stopping further investment. If you started without market contact, the review reflects first-outreach evidence and direction—not a final market verdict.

Between sessions

We use a shared Miro or Mural board as the working space for organising the information you share, mapping evidence, recording questions, prioritising decisions and tracking agreed actions. We review your information, ask clarifying questions by email and update the board before each online meeting. Within five working days after each session, we send a written summary and next-step list; any outstanding points are carried into the next scheduled discussion. Substantive discussions and decisions happen in the scheduled sessions.

What we need from you

One hour each week for the scheduled online session, access to relevant information (such as distributor reports, pipeline extracts, shipment or installation records and hospital contact details), and willingness to carry out agreed actions between sessions. The time required for actions depends on the challenge and may increase during the Test phase.

What you receive

  • Written diagnosis and prioritised list of accounts, opportunities or issues.
  • Action plan with owners and dates.
  • Record of actions and customer or distributor feedback.
  • End-of-sprint decision review: continue, change or stop.
  • Practical 90-day priorities.

Not included

Introductions to hospitals or distributors, or execution of sales, marketing or distributor activity on your behalf.

The sprint supports better decisions and disciplined execution. It does not guarantee sales or purchase orders.
