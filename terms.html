10-week go-to-market sprint and payment

The Free 30-Minute GTM Clarity Call is an introductory online conversation. If KawaHive is a suitable fit, we will review the information you choose to share and prepare a high-level GTM action plan, usually within about one week. There is no obligation to proceed with paid support.

The 10-Week Go-to-Market Sprint has a fixed fee of US$3,500 for ten weeks, payable in full before the sprint begins. The sprint focuses on one primary GTM challenge and includes one scheduled 60-minute online session each week. Between sessions, KawaHive reviews information shared by the client, asks clarifying questions by email and updates a shared working board before the next online meeting. A written session summary and next-step list will be sent within five working days after each session.

The client may cancel at no charge before both the start date and first session are confirmed. Once both are confirmed, the sprint is non-cancellable and non-refundable. Sessions may be rescheduled without charge with at least five business days’ notice. Less than five business days’ notice incurs a US$380 short-notice rescheduling fee per session, payable before the rescheduled session. A missed session without prior rescheduling counts as delivered and cannot be refunded or rebooked. If KawaHive needs to reschedule, a replacement date will be arranged at no cost.

Any work requested beyond the ten-week sprint is scoped separately, with the scope and fee agreed before that work begins. The sprint supports better decisions and disciplined execution; it does not guarantee sales, purchase orders or market-entry results.
