Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are you a regulatory consultancy?

No. We do not prepare regulatory submissions or provide regulatory consulting or compliance services. We focus on go-to-market decisions: customer prioritisation, market choices, distributor performance, positioning and the steps that can turn regulatory progress into customer traction.

Are you a marketing agency?

No. We do not sell marketing campaigns or act as an outsourced marketing team. We help you understand the go-to-market problem first — for example, why hospital interest is not converting into purchase orders, or why a distributor is not generating sustained demand — and clarify which decisions to address next.

Do I need to have chosen my country and distributor?

No. We can help you assess your options. The free Free MedTech Prospecting Training with ICP Scorecard will be most useful once you have a target market or customer segment in mind.

What are your payment and cancellation terms?

The 10-week sprint has a fixed fee of US$3,500, payable in full before it begins. You may cancel at no charge before both the start date and first session are confirmed. Once both are confirmed, the sprint is non-cancellable and non-refundable. Sessions can be rescheduled free of charge with at least five business days’ notice. Less than five business days’ notice incurs a US$380 short-notice rescheduling fee per session, payable before the rescheduled session. A missed session without prior rescheduling counts as delivered and cannot be refunded or rebooked. If KawaHive needs to reschedule, a replacement date will be arranged at no cost.

Can I join if my product is still awaiting regulatory approval?

The sprint is for products or services already authorised to operate or be sold in the target market. While you are waiting, you can use the free tools and Insights in KawaHive Lab. You are welcome to book a call once approval is in place.

Do I need existing hospital or distributor contacts?

No. The sprint can start with founders who already have hospital or distributor activity but few orders, and founders with approval but no market contact yet. We will discuss whether the sprint is the right fit during the free call.

What’s slowing your go-to-market progress?

Talk it through with us — free, no obligation. We’ll help you identify the real issue and the decision that needs attention first.
