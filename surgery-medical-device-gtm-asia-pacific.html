Go-to-market planning for surgery devices

Surgical equipment and operating room technologies may be evaluated by surgeons, operating theatre teams, nursing leaders, biomedical engineering, procurement and hospital management. The buying route can differ by product, hospital and country. KawaHive helps founders with a registered MedTech or medical device product or service understand the customer journey and focus go-to-market activity on the accounts and decisions that matter.

Our focus includes surgical equipment, instruments and operating room technologies. The right market, customer segment and buying route depend on the product, country and care setting. We help teams test assumptions, prioritise activity and make next steps explicit.

Surgical buying considerations to validate

For surgical equipment and operating room technologies, adoption may depend on more than surgeon interest. Theatre workflow, compatibility with existing equipment, sterilisation or reprocessing requirements where relevant, staff training, service support and scheduling for evaluation can influence the decision. The account plan should identify the clinical champion, operational users, technical reviewers and budget owner, then document what each stakeholder needs before the hospital can proceed.

Where go-to-market progress can get stuck

  • Prioritise surgical specialties, hospitals and accounts that align with the product's intended use and value proposition.
  • Map clinical champions, theatre users, technical evaluators and budget or procurement decision-makers.
  • Assess distributor access to relevant departments and ability to support demonstrations, training coordination and follow-up.
  • Make the product's value proposition relevant to clinical workflow, service requirements and purchasing priorities.
  • Define measurable next steps from product introduction through evaluation, budget consideration and purchase.

Questions to answer before scaling activity

  • Which surgical teams and procedures are the intended initial focus?
  • Who evaluates usability, workflow fit, technical requirements and budget?
  • What local support is needed before and after a product evaluation?
  • What is the documented next step and owner for each target account?
