About KawaHive

Commercial clarity for MedTech and medical device founders

Regulatory clearance is a milestone, not a commercial strategy. Hospital interest and a distributor agreement do not automatically lead to purchase orders.

KawaHive & Partners helps MedTech and medical device founders address these challenges across Asia-Pacific.

We work with teams that have a product and regulatory progress but need stronger commercial traction. Common issues include:

  • Clinical interest that fails to convert into purchase orders
  • Distributors who hold stock but generate limited sustained demand
  • Uncertainty regarding priority markets or customer segments
  • Strong clinical value that does not translate into commercial results

We listen first, ask focused questions, and assess the commercial issue. We will be clear about whether we can help. Any paid work is scoped and agreed upfront, and there is no obligation after the discovery session.

We bring 20 years of medical device experience from companies including GE, Fujifilm, Pentax and Konica Minolta, with commercial work across Asia-Pacific.

We do not provide regulatory consulting or marketing services. We focus on customer priorities, distributor performance, positioning and commercial decisions.

Start with a free discovery session. If we are a fit, we’ll agree the next step together.

We’re here to help you move from symptoms to the commercial decisions that matter.
