About KawaHive

Industry experience. APAC understanding. Practical go-to-market support.

KawaHive & Partners is a new advisory practice built on more than 20 years of experience in MedTech and Medical Devicess across Asia-Pacific.

We help founders turn market understanding into practical go-to-market decisions — and move beyond distributor appointments, hospital interest and product demonstrations towards customer orders.

Our focus is helping founders with a registered product or service strengthen their APAC go-to-market strategy. We look at which markets to prioritise, which customers and stakeholders to engage, how distributors are performing and what may be preventing customer interest from converting into orders. We focus on the practical steps between product or service registration and sustained sales traction.

Our industry experience spans six market segments: Radiology, Surgery, OBGYN, Urology, Rehabilitation and Gastroenterology. These indicate areas of relevant familiarity, not separate KawaHive service lines.

We work with teams that have made progress towards market readiness but need a clearer, more effective go-to-market strategy. Common challenges include:

  • Clinical interest that fails to convert into purchase orders
  • Distributors who hold stock but generate limited sustained demand
  • Uncertainty regarding priority markets or customer segments
  • Strong clinical value that does not translate into customer orders

How we work

We start with a Free 30-Minute GTM Clarity Call: a 30-minute online conversation to understand your situation and assess whether KawaHive is the right fit. Where we can help, we prepare a high-level GTM action plan in approximately one week, depending on the information you share. You can use the plan independently or choose to work with us through our fixed-fee 10-week GTM Sprint (US$3,500). If we are not the right fit, we will recommend someone better suited at no cost.

Who we work with

We work with teams whose MedTech and Medical Devicess are authorised in their target market and who need to turn market activity into customer orders. The challenge may be hospital interest without purchase orders, distributor stock without sell-through, unclear market priorities or no customer conversations yet.

We are not suited to companies awaiting product approval or seeking regulatory, clinical-evidence or marketing services. Free tools and Insights are available while approval is pending.

Experience behind the practice

The practice draws on prior professional roles with established organisations including GE Healthcare, Fujifilm, Pentax and Konica Minolta. These are previous industry backgrounds, not KawaHive client engagements.

This experience informs our perspective on healthcare markets, customer engagement, product adoption, distributor relationships and go-to-market execution across diverse APAC markets.

We combine industry experience with structured learning and practical GTM thinking. Rather than applying a one-size-fits-all formula, we work to understand each business, its customers, its route to market and the specific barriers standing between interest and orders.

We do not provide clinical, regulatory or technical certification advice. We focus on customer priorities, distributor performance, positioning and go-to-market decisions.

Our US$3,500 fixed-fee 10-week GTM Sprint provides weekly online sessions, review of founder-shared information, email clarification between sessions and a shared working board updated before each meeting. Written session summaries and next-step lists are sent within five working days. Any work beyond ten weeks is scoped and priced separately before it begins.

We help you move from symptoms to the go-to-market decisions that matter.
